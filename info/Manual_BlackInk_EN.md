## Tattoo Styles

### 1. **PHOTOREALISTIC**
Tattoos that replicate photographs with extreme precision. Includes portraits, animals, objects.
- **Complexity:** High
- **Time:** 3-8 hours
- **Color maintenance:** Touch-up at 3 years
- **Recommended for:** People with tattoo experience

### 2. **FINELINE (FINE LINE)**
Delicate designs with ultrafine and precise lines. Ideal for small tattoos.
- **Complexity:** Medium-High
- **Time:** 1-3 hours
- **Maintenance:** Touch-up every 5-7 years
- **Recommended for:** First tattoos, people seeking subtlety

### 3. **OLDSCHOOL AMERICAN**
Classic style with vibrant colors, thick lines, and iconic designs.
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Time:** 2-4 hours
- **Maintenance:** Touch-up every 10 years approximately
- **Recommended for:** Lovers of classic tattoo culture

### 4. **TRIBAL**
Geometric and symbolic designs inspired by ancestral cultures.
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Time:** 2-5 hours
- **Maintenance:** Black ink remains permanent
- **Recommended for:** People seeking cultural meaning

### 5. **BIOMECHANICAL**
Fusion of futuristic mechanics with organic elements. Looks like metal emerges from skin.
- **Complexity:** High
- **Time:** 4-8 hours (may require multiple sessions)
- **Maintenance:** Touch-up every 3-5 years
- **Recommended for:** People seeking unique, futuristic designs

### 6. **WATERCOLOR**
Tattoos that imitate watercolor painting technique with soft and diffused colors.
- **Complexity:** High
- **Time:** 2-4 hours
- **Maintenance:** Touch-up every 2-3 years due to color degradation
- **Recommended for:** Art lovers and creative people

### 7. **GOTHIC AND FANTASY**
Dark, magical, and fantastic designs with gothic culture elements.
- **Complexity:** High
- **Time:** 3-6 hours
- **Maintenance:** Black ink remains, detail touch-up every 5 years
- **Recommended for:** People with taste for dark and artistic things

### 8. **MINIMALIST**
Simple, geometric, and clean designs with minimum lines.
- **Complexity:** Low-Medium
- **Time:** 30 minutes - 2 hours
- **Maintenance:** Touch-up every 5-10 years
- **Recommended for:** First tattoos, discreet people

### 9. **MANDALAS**
Circular and symmetrical designs inspired by spirituality.
- **Complexity:** Medium-High
- **Time:** 2-4 hours
- **Maintenance:** Touch-up every 5-7 years
- **Recommended for:** People seeking balance and spirituality

### 10. **LETTERS AND CALLIGRAPHY**
Names, phrases, or quotes with customized typographic styles.
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Time:** 1-3 hours
- **Maintenance:** Touch-up every 5-10 years
- **Recommended for:** People wanting to carry a meaningful message

## Post-Tattoo Care

### FIRST 24 HOURS (CRITICAL PHASE)

#### Immediately after

1. **Keep the bandage on:** Minimum 2-3 hours. Protects from environment.
2. **Wash carefully:** With warm water and gentle neutral soap.
3. **Dry carefully:** With disposable paper towels (NOT fabric towels).
4. **Apply antibiotic cream:** Thin layer of antibiotic ointment recommended by artist.
5. **Don't cover immediately:** Let skin breathe 15-20 minutes.
6. **Repeat every 2-3 hours:** Wash, dry, cream.

#### What to AVOID the first 24 hours

- ❌ Don't unnecessarily wet the tattoo
- ❌ Don't touch with dirty hands
- ❌ Don't share personal items (towels, etc.)
- ❌ Don't wear very tight clothing over tattoo
- ❌ Don't do intense sports
- ❌ Don't go to pools or beach
- ❌ Don't sleep directly on the tattoo
- ❌ Don't apply ice compresses directly

### DAYS 2-7 (SCABBING PHASE)

#### Daily routine

1. **Wash 2-3 times daily:** Warm water + neutral soap, dry thoroughly.
2. **Apply cream or balm:** Every time after washing.
3. **Keep hydrating constantly:** Skin will start to flake, this is normal.
4. **Avoid scratching:** Even if it itches, DON'T scratch. Use cool compresses if itching is intense.

#### Special care

- Wear loose clothing over tattoo
- Sleep in position that doesn't rub tattoo
- If you sweat heavily, clean gently
- Don't expose to direct sun
- Keep skin clean but don't over-wash

#### Recommended products

- **Healing balm:** Tattoo-specific (Bepantol, Sudocrem)
- **Antibiotic cream:** Neosporin or similar
- **Neutral soap:** Suitable for sensitive skin
- **Sunscreen:** SPF 50+ after 2 weeks

### WEEK 2-4 (DEEP HEALING)

#### Expected changes

- Tattoo will look "opaque" temporarily (normal)
- Skin will have mostly flaked
- Color will look paler (will regain shine in weeks)
- May remain slightly swollen

#### Ongoing care

1. **Keep hydrating:** 1-2 times daily with balm or cream
2. **Sun protection:** Crucial. SPF 50+, apply every 2 hours if outdoors
3. **Avoid prolonged sun:** For 4 weeks minimum
4. **Don't go to pools/beach:** Until week 4
5. **No intense sports:** Until week 3
6. **Don't pressure tattoo while sleeping**

#### Warning signs

If you observe any of these symptoms, contact the studio:
- ⚠️ Excessive redness or swelling
- ⚠️ Pus or unusual fluid discharge
- ⚠️ Intense heat in the area
- ⚠️ Red lines radiating from tattoo
- ⚠️ Allergic reaction (intense itching, rash)
- ⚠️ Fever

### AFTER 4 WEEKS (FINAL PHASE)

#### Healed tattoo

The tattoo should be completely healed in 4 weeks. If you still see flaking or swelling, continue with care.

#### Long-term maintenance

- **Daily hydration:** Apply neutral moisturizing cream
- **Sun protection always:** UV rays fade colors
- **Avoid trauma:** Don't scratch or hit the area
- **Comfortable clothing:** Especially in first 2 weeks
- **Keep skin healthy:** General body hydration

### COMMON REACTIONS

#### Flaking and itching (NORMAL)
- **Duration:** Days 3-7 typically
- **Cause:** Natural healing process
- **Solution:** Keep area hydrated, DON'T scratch

#### Mild swelling (NORMAL)
- **Duration:** First 3-5 days
- **Cause:** Normal inflammatory response
- **Solution:** Cool compresses (NO direct ice), keep clean

#### Color looks pale temporarily (NORMAL)
- **Duration:** 2-4 weeks
- **Cause:** Swollen skin masks true color
- **Solution:** Be patient, color will return to brilliance

#### Persistent intense itching (CONSULT)
- **Possible cause:** Pigment allergy
- **Action:** Contact studio, may require medical follow-up

#### Redness or heat (CONSULT)
- **Possible cause:** Infection
- **Action:** Contact studio immediately
- **Prevention:** Keep clean, don't touch with dirty hands

## Frequently Asked Questions

### DOES IT HURT TO GET A TATTOO?

**Yes, there is pain, but it's tolerable.**

The level varies depending on:
- **Body area:** Areas with more bone (ribs, spine) hurt more
- **Your pain threshold:** Varies person to person
- **Size and complexity:** Larger tattoos = more time = accumulated pain
- **Tattoo artist:** Technique and experience matter

**Pain comparison by area (scale 1-10):**
- Outer arm: 4-5
- Forearm: 4-5
- Outer thigh: 4-5
- Ribs: 8-9 (MOST PAINFUL)
- Spine: 8-9 (MOST PAINFUL)
- Chest: 6-7
- Calf: 5-6
- Foot: 7-8

**Recommendations:**
- Bring music or podcasts for distraction
- Breathe deeply during intense sessions
- Eat something before (but not too much)
- Sleep well the night before
- Tell artist if pain becomes unbearable

### HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO HEAL?

**External healing:** 2-3 weeks
**Complete healing:** 4-6 weeks
**Final stabilization:** 3-4 months

During this time, color may look pale. This is normal. Once healing completes, color will regain brilliance.

### CAN I GET THE TATTOO WET?

**First 24 hours:** Avoid unnecessary wetting. Yes to quick washing, no to soaking.

**First 2 weeks:** Quick showers are okay. Avoid:
- Long baths
- Pools
- Beaches
- Hot tubs

**After 2 weeks:** You can wet normally, but protect with sunscreen if there's sun.

### WHAT IF I REGRET IT?

Black Ink Studio offers options:

1. **Cover-up:** We design new tattoo covering the old one (€200-€600)
2. **Partial removal:** If tattoo is small (€150-€300)
3. **Modification:** We alter existing design (€100-€400)

**Professional laser removers:** We don't operate lasers, but recommend associated clinics.

### CAN I GET TATTOOED DURING PREGNANCY?

**We don't recommend tattoos during pregnancy** because:
- Potential infection risk (though low with proper protocols)
- Body changes, tattoo may distort
- Better to wait post-pregnancy (after nursing if applicable)

**After pregnancy:** Recommend waiting 3-4 months post-delivery and 6 months if breastfeeding.

### DO TATTOOS AFFECT BLOOD DONATIONS?

In many countries, **you can donate blood**, but:
- Wait 12 months after tattoo (varies by country)
- If done in certified studio like Black Ink, usually no restriction
- Check with local blood bank

In Spain and EU: Most authorities allow donation after professional tattoo with 4-12 week wait.

### WHEN DO I NEED A TOUCH-UP?

Depends on style:
- **Simple black ink:** 7-10 years
- **Oldschool/vibrant colors:** 10-15 years
- **Watercolor/gradients:** 3-5 years
- **Realism:** 3-5 years

Touch-ups keep tattoo fresh and prevent fading.

### CAN I BE IN THE SUN AFTER GETTING TATTOOED?

**First 4 weeks:** Avoid direct sun exposure. UV rays slow healing.

**After 4 weeks:** Always use SPF 50+ sunscreen. UV rays permanently fade tattoos.

### WHAT IF I HAVE AN ALLERGIC REACTION?

**Symptoms:**
- Intense and persistent itching
- Rash or hives around area
- Abnormal swelling

**Actions:**
1. Contact Black Ink Studio immediately
2. Don't apply products without consulting
3. See dermatologist if severe
4. Take antihistamine if necessary

Allergic reactions to pigments are rare (less than 1%) with quality certified pigments.

### CAN I EXERCISE?

- **First 3-5 days:** Avoid intense exercise (increases sweating and swelling)
- **First week:** Light exercise is fine
- **After 1 week:** Normal exercise, avoid tight clothing rubbing tattoo

## Tips and Recommendations

### BEFORE COMING TO THE STUDIO

#### 1. Prepare mentally
- Visualize the tattoo
- Remember why you want it
- Accept it's permanent (or semi-permanent with right design)
- Prepare for long session (may last several hours)

#### 2. Sleep well the night before
- Get adequate rest (7-8 hours)
- You'll arrive more relaxed and with better pain tolerance

#### 3. Eat nutritious food before
- Eat good breakfast/lunch 1-2 hours before
- Sugar and protein will give you energy
- Arrive hydrated

#### 4. Don't consume stimulants
- ❌ DON'T drink alcohol 24 hours before (thins blood)
- ❌ DON'T take aspirin (anticoagulant)
- ❌ DON'T smoke excessively (restricts blood vessels)
- ✅ OKAY: Coffee in normal amounts (but not excessive)

#### 5. Dress comfortably
- Loose clothing that doesn't press tattoo area
- Avoid tight pants/shirts
- Comfortable shoes

#### 6. Bring clear references
- Images of style you want
- Photos of specific elements
- Detailed descriptions
- Clearer = better result

#### 7. Arrive punctually
- 15 minutes early
- Allows artist to prepare everything
- Relax your mind before starting

### DURING THE SESSION

#### 1. Communicate
- Inform about sensitivities
- Tell if you need to stop
- Ask about technique
- Artist appreciates feedback

#### 2. Stay calm
- Deep, slow breathing
- Focus on relaxing muscles
- Avoid tensing the area
- Tension increases pain

#### 3. Distract your mind
- Bring headphones with music/podcasts
- Focus on something different
- Talk with artist if it helps
- Some clients bring laptop to watch shows

#### 4. Stay hydrated
- Drink water constantly
- Replenish fluids from sweating
- Helps your body process better

#### 5. Take breaks if necessary
- For long sessions (4+ hours), it's normal to request pause
- Artist will schedule breaks
- Use time to recover

### AFTER LEAVING THE STUDIO

#### First hours

1. **Keep bandage on:** Minimum 2-3 hours
2. **Clean gently:** Warm water + neutral soap
3. **Dry well:** With disposable paper
4. **Apply cream:** Thin layer of balm
5. **Let breathe:** 15-20 minutes without covers

#### Day 1

- Wash every 2-3 hours
- Apply cream after each wash
- Avoid tight clothing
- Don't sleep directly on tattoo
- Keep away from prolonged water

#### Following weeks

- Consistent cleaning routine
- Daily hydration
- Sun protection
- Don't scratch even if itchy intensely

### LIFESTYLE AND TATTOOS

#### Diet
- **Protein:** Needed for healing. Eat chicken, fish, eggs
- **Vitamin C:** Strengthens collagen. Oranges, strawberries, peppers
- **Water:** Stay hydrated (2+ liters daily)
- **Avoid:** Excessive alcohol, ultra-processed food

#### Exercise
- Wait 3-5 days before intense exercise
- Avoid sweat "trapped" on tattoo
- After 1 week, resume normal activities
- Avoid tight clothing rubbing tattoo

#### Sleep
- Sleep well (7-8 hours)
- Body heals during sleep
- Sleep in position that doesn't press tattoo
- Use clean pillows

#### Stress
- Reduce stress during healing
- Stress slows healing
- Practice meditation or yoga
- Keep positive mindset

### LONG-TERM CARE

#### Keep skin healthy
1. **Hydrate daily:** Neutral cream or lotion
2. **Protect from sun:** SPF 50+ always (especially over tattoo)
3. **Don't smoke:** Smoke ages skin
4. **Sleep well:** 7-8 hours recharge your skin
5. **Maintain stable weight:** Radical changes distort tattoo

#### Touch-ups
- Schedule touch-ups every 5-10 years depending on style
- Keep tattoo bright and defined
- Usually faster (1-2 hours)
- Cost: 50% of original price

#### Modifications
- Want to change tattoo: cover-up or modification
- Want to expand: Black Ink can integrate elements
- Share ideas with artist for harmony

### MYTHS ABOUT TATTOOS (DEBUNKED)

❌ **"Tattoos completely fade"**
✅ FALSE: With proper care, they last decades. Occasional touch-up keeps them fresh.

❌ **"Tattoos affect employability"**
✅ PARTIALLY: Depends on industry. Many modern companies accept tattoos. Consider placement.

❌ **"All tattoos get infected"**
✅ FALSE: With hygiene protocols (like Black Ink Studio), infection is rare (<1%).

❌ **"Tattoos hurt the same everywhere"**
✅ FALSE: Hurts more on bone areas, less on fleshy areas.

❌ **"Can't tattoo dark skin"**
✅ FALSE: You can, but requires special technique. Consult experienced artists.

❌ **"Tattoos are permanent forever"**
✅ FALSE: Laser removers exist. Also modifications and cover-ups.

### FINAL ADVICE

A tattoo is a permanent (or semi-permanent) commitment to art on your body. Take time to:
- Choose the right design
- Select the right artist
- Understand the commitment
- Follow care instructions carefully
